# Fork Policy

## License Compliance Requirement

All forks of this repository **must** update to the new license and comply with its terms. This policy is binding for all current and future forks.

## Requirements for Forks

When the license is updated in the upstream repository, every fork is required to:

1. **Pull the latest license changes** from upstream within 30 days of the upstream license update
2. **Update their LICENSE file** to match the upstream LICENSE exactly
3. **Ensure all distributed copies** comply with the new license terms
4. **Maintain attribution notices** as required by the license
5. **Not remove or modify** the license compliance requirements from this document

## Automatic Enforcement

- GitHub's fork sync feature should be enabled to receive upstream license updates
- CI/CD pipelines should verify LICENSE file matches upstream
- Any distribution (releases, packages, deployments) must include the current license

## Non-Compliance

Forks that fail to update their license within 30 days of an upstream license change:

- Lose the right to distribute the software under the previous license terms
- Must immediately cease distribution until compliant
- May be subject to DMCA takedown for unauthorized distribution

## Rationale

This policy ensures:
- Legal clarity for all users and contributors
- Consistent license terms across the ecosystem
- Protection of contributor rights
- Compliance with open source licensing obligations

## Questions

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the upstream repository.
